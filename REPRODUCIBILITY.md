Step3 v1.4, Step4 v1.1, Step4.5 v1.4, Step5 v1.0 and Step5B v1.1.

large source inputs and an appropriately configured environment.

scores without risk-group discrepancies. These 240 historical cases are distinct from the
254 source-reconstructed GSE16011 cases used in the revised analysis. An independent Cox
refit reproduced the nine coefficients within 6.7e-16; selected ML0133 configuration replay
matched all fold-specific gene rankings and C-indices within 2.3e-16. Exact source-gate checks
preserved all 245 cleaned and 245 raw gene-tier memberships. These are retained-input checks,
not a complete raw-input reproduction or an unbiased fully nested validation.

## Interpretation

Numerical reproduction is distinct from independent biological or clinical validation.

analyses did not establish score-guided drug selection. See the manuscript for full estimates.
